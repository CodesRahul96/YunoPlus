## 1.0.0

### Initial Release of Yuno+
- **Playback:** Ad-free streaming up to 4K (2160p) and 1440p 60fps with VP9, DASH/HLS audio-video merging, and background audio playback.
- **Player Controls:** Rich on-video controls with ±10s skips, scrub preview, speed control, zoom-to-fill, and SponsorBlock integration.
- **Mini-Player:** Floating liquid glass mini-player dock with instant Play/Pause and quick Close (`✕`) button.
- **Status Bar Controls:** Android notification media card with integrated Close action for background playback.
- **Notifications Page:** Dedicated subscribed channel notifications screen with login state detection.
- **Shorts Feed:** Vertical Shorts feed with disk-cached instant playback.
- **Liquid Glass Navigation:** Translucent navigation bar with backdrop blur and smooth spring physics.
- **Account Sync:** Optional Google sign-in with encrypted session storage (Android Keystore).
